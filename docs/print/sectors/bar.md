# Visible design bar: A3 sector atlas, revision 2

Reference: user-approved KWS app map, codex-clipboard-b486e08a-ddaa-4438-8f2e-5b813fc9a2bf.png. It supplies the map material/lighting direction, not a literal app-screen layout. Format, content and governing files were already specified by the user: A3, all sectors with names/positions/QRs, app design in docs/DESIGN.md. No new interview needed for these settled inputs.

1. A3 portrait. Map centered, at least 280 mm high; it is the single dominant illustration, not a thumbnail next to a directory table.
2. Off-white edges and a diffuse pale sage floor. White wall tops, visible shallow gray sides, soft contact shadows. No colored sector fills or gray rounded container around the map.
3. One strong Teko headline (at least 60 pt), Poppins for labels, two font families maximum. Body navy #192436, accent #36B531 unchanged.
4. Exactly 19 unique sector QR targets. All sector captions present. Small association numbers may connect markers to QR captions. No table rules, column headings, repeated rounded cards or technical counters.
5. QR side columns separated from the center illustration. Every code at least 23 mm with four white quiet-zone modules; caption at least 9 pt. Code/caption pairs stay together.
6. Public group names placed in their actual map vicinity; A-D markers anchored to source polygons. Bug A and Kurze Platte distinct. No invented geography, entrance or wall heights.
7. Safe margins at least 8 mm; header, code columns, map and footer do not overlap. Footer has one plain scanning instruction and readable app address.

Critics receive rendered output; brief/system/craft are independent. Poster creation is fixed-format print work, not a responsive app implementation.
