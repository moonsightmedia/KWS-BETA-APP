# KWS poster constraints

Brand: original green #36B531, strong navy #192436; white and pale sage/neutral surfaces. Green text on pale surfaces uses darker ink #237D20. No unrelated accent palette.

Typography: Teko semibold for primary title, Poppins regular/semibold elsewhere. Original KWS logo, without stretched aspect ratio.

Map: saved production polygons. White architectural walls, shallow schematic gray depth, soft contact shadows, neutral sage floor. Preserve geographic relationships. Labels may be repositioned for clarity; physical sector anchors remain sourced. Kurze Platte independent from Bug A. Marker letters A-D remain clearly readable.

Confirmed physical geography (user correction, docs/DESIGN.md section 7.4):
the long narrow wall directly LEFT of Bug is Kurze Platte. The upper wedge
immediately to its RIGHT is Bug A. These neighboring walls share the open floor;
there is no physical divider between their floor-label areas. Kurze Platte's
saved marker anchor is toward the upper end of that left strip. Adjacency to Bug
is therefore correct geography, not a grouping error. The unique 19/A marker
and 19 Kurze Platte QR caption identify it independently; never move this
physical anchor into a different wall merely to place it nearer a floor label.

Print: A3 297 x 420 mm plus optional 3 mm bleed. All informative content at least 8 mm inside trim. Vector QR codes, minimum 23 mm, four modules white quiet zone. Navy on white. All 19 public names and production QR targets retained. RGB disclosed, no unverified CMYK claim.

Small printed association numbers 01–19 pair the repeated A–D wall letters with
their exact QR/name caption. They are wayfinding aids, not new sector IDs, counts
or an alternative public naming system. Retain the letter and public name.

No copy of app navigation, no screenshot of app chrome on the poster. No card-in-card framing or pill-shaped labels. Two font families. One obvious headline and one dominant map illustration.
