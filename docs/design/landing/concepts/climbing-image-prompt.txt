Use case: ui-mockup.
Create one high-fidelity FLAT mobile app screen concept for the planned rope-climbing area of the German KWS Beta App. This is the DISPLAY CONTENT ONLY, not a phone product photograph.
Reference image 1 (app-native-boulders.webp) is the primary visual-style reference: match its navy condensed Teko-style heading, Poppins-like body typography, white / very light gray-green background, green #36B531 selection, crisp small-radius white route cards and simple fine outline icons.
Reference image 2 (roadmap-app-training.webp) is a supporting scale and mobile UI reference from the same app. Do not copy its training illustrations.
Reference image 3 (hall.webp) is a supporting real Kletterwelt Sauerland interior photo; use it as a wide scenic photo near the top of the screen.
Composition: one tall portrait app viewport, approximately 780 pixels wide by 1600 pixels high (aspect 0.4875), flat orthographic edge-to-edge UI, no margins outside the screen. No device bezel, no black border, no Dynamic Island and NO SYSTEM STATUS BAR: those will be added by our existing website phone frame.
Screen design: at the top a compact back chevron and large condensed navy title "Kletterhalle", with small neutral line "Konzeptvorschau" underneath and simple search/filter icons at right. A compact horizontal filter row with "Alle" selected in bright green with navy text, then "Toprope" and "Vorstieg". Below it a generous panoramic crop of the supplied climbing hall photo with subtle small caption "Routen entdecken". Beneath the image place three clean route list cards with small climbing-hold photo thumbnails, a navy sample route name, secondary gray rope-climbing discipline, grade tags and right chevrons. Exact sample labels: "Grüne Linie" / "Toprope" / "5+", "Sonnenkante" / "Vorstieg" / "6-", "Blaue Stunde" / "Toprope" / "6". These are clearly hypothetical sample routes for the concept.
At the bottom use a compact white app tab bar matching the references, with four thin outline icons and labels "Home", "Boulder", "Klettern", "Profil". Klettern is selected in green with navy icon and label. Keep the layout visually balanced and readable, with realistic app spacing and equal card rhythm.
Must preserve: existing KWS visual language, German spelling including umlauts, existing green/navy palette. Avoid: invented availability dates, statistics or user accounts, brand-logo redraws, decorative 3D objects, gradients, glass, large rounded pills, training cartoons, phone hardware, extra annotations outside the screen. This is a plausible new interface concept, not a claim that the feature is implemented.
